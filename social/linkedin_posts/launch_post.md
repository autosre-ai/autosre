## Post Content

🚀 **Announcing AutoSRE: Open-Source Autonomous Incident Response**

After 18 months of development and battle-testing in production environments, I'm thrilled to announce that AutoSRE is now open source.

**What is AutoSRE?**

AutoSRE is an AI-powered autonomous incident response system that diagnoses, triages, and remediates production incidents automatically. Think of it as having a tireless, expert SRE watching your infrastructure 24/7.

**Why we built this:**

Every SRE team I've worked with faces the same challenges:
• Alert fatigue from noisy monitoring
• 3 AM pages for issues that have known solutions
• Institutional knowledge trapped in runbooks nobody reads
• Growing infrastructure complexity outpacing team capacity

We asked: what if AI could handle the 80% of incidents that follow known patterns, so humans could focus on the 20% that actually require creativity?

**How it works:**

AutoSRE uses a multi-agent AI architecture where specialized agents collaborate:

🔍 Diagnostic Agent — Investigates and identifies root cause
🔗 Correlation Agent — Connects related signals across your stack
🛠️ Remediation Agent — Executes safe, reversible fixes
📚 Learning Agent — Continuously improves from every incident

**Key Features:**

✅ Kubernetes-native with first-class K8s support
✅ Integrates with PagerDuty, Slack, Prometheus, and more
✅ Human-in-the-loop for critical decisions
✅ Full audit trail and explainability
✅ Extensible with custom agents and runbooks

**Early Results:**

Organizations using AutoSRE in beta have seen:
• 73% reduction in Mean Time to Resolution
• 85% fewer after-hours pages
• 90% of routine incidents auto-resolved

**Open Source & Free Forever:**

We believe the future of infrastructure operations should be open. AutoSRE is MIT licensed and free to use, forever.

🔗 GitHub: github.com/autosre/autosre
📖 Documentation: docs.autosre.io
💬 Community: discord.gg/autosre

**Get Started:**

```
pip install autosre
autosre init
autosre run
```
